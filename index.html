TACTICAL GAME GUIDE

Awake inside Sector 7 of a high-security subterranean facility following an experimental containment breach. Infiltrate sector corridors, bypass armed security guard patrols and flying drones, collect energy crystals, and retrieve sector keycards before automated facility lockdown!

FEATURE SPECIFICATIONS

  • 2.5D Canvas Engine: Extruded platform blocks, depth lighting & real-world parallax backgrounds.
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+): Ultra-fast 60 FPS physics without external dependencies.
  • Web Audio SFX: Dynamic synthesized audio generator for realistic laser traps & movement.
  • Mobile Optimized: Full touch controls & responsive orientation handling.
