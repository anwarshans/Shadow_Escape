ABOUT SHADOW ESCAPE

In Sector 7 of an abandoned subterranean research laboratory, an experimental containment breach triggered automated lockdowns. You play as a human runner persona escaping human security guard patrols, flying drones, and laser traps across 5 stages.

TECH STACK HIGHLIGHTS

  • HTML5 Canvas: 2.5D block projections & depth shadows.
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+): Zero external game engine dependencies.
  • Web Audio API: Dynamic synthesized audio generator for SFX and synthwave BGM.
  • Uploads Directory: Custom PNG sprite assets for human runner, human security guards, and hero portrait.
